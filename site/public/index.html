A Sip of Yemen, Steeped in Tradition

Qahwah Drip brings the coffee of our homeland to Falls Church. Coffee was first cultivated in the mountains of Yemen centuries ago, and we carry that legacy into every cup — roasted with care, brewed slowly, and shared generously across the greater DMV.

Rooted in Yemen

Heritage in Every Pour

Our walls hold the terraced highlands of Yemen — where coffee was born and where our family's story begins. From the port of Mokha to the peaks of Haraz, this is the land that gave the world its first cup.

We honor it with traditional recipes, slow rituals, and the hospitality of a Sana'a sitting room.
