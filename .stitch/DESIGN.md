# Qahwah Drip — Design System

**Brand:** Qahwah Drip — *"Coffee, Teas & Desserts."* A modern Yemeni coffee house bringing
the heritage of Yemeni *qahwah* to a warm, premium, contemporary space.

## Brand essence
Warm, hospitable, premium, rooted in Yemeni heritage. Old-Sana'a craftsmanship meets a clean
modern café: marble + brass + walnut, anchored by a confident scarlet.

## Color palette
- **Qahwah Red `#C4202C`** — primary brand color (logo, CTAs, key accents). Bold, warm scarlet.
- **Brass Gold `#B8924A`** — secondary; arabesque lattice, hairline dividers, fine detail. Accents only, never large fills.
- **Highland Green `#2E6B43`** — tertiary; sparing nods to Yemen's terraced mountains.
- **Marble Cream `#F6F1E8`** — primary light background (warm Carrara marble).
- **Pure Linen `#FBF8F2`** — raised surfaces / cards.
- **Walnut `#5A3E29`** — wood tones, footer, deep sections.
- **Camel Tan `#B98A5E`** — leather / banquette warmth.
- **Espresso Ink `#2A211B`** — primary text on light.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Playfair Display — elegant high-contrast serif echoing the logo wordmark.
- **Body:** Nunito Sans — warm, rounded, highly readable.
- **Labels / Nav:** Montserrat, uppercase, wide tracking for small UI labels.
- Tasteful Arabic word accents (قهوة) where they add authenticity.

## Shape & texture
- Soft 8px roundness on cards & buttons.
- **Signature motif: the pointed Islamic arch (mihrab)** framing hero images and feature cards — key images arch upward at the top.
- **Arabesque quatrefoil lattice** (brass on marble) as section dividers and subtle backgrounds.
- 8-point Islamic star geometry as small ornaments.
- Brass hairline rules separating sections.

## Layout
- Generous whitespace on a marble-light canvas, content max-width ~1200px.
- Full-bleed image heroes with a soft dark gradient so white headlines stay legible.
- Asymmetric editorial sections (image left / text right, alternating).
- Sticky header: transparent over the hero, turns solid marble on scroll.

## Imagery
Real photography of the café — marble + brass lattice wall, espresso pulls, traditional handleless
qahwah cups, Yemeni mountain-village murals, mashrabiya arches, green-and-white bistro patio.
Warm natural light. No generic stock.

## Motion
Subtle: gentle fade-up on scroll, slow hero zoom, smooth 200ms hover lifts.

## Voice
Warm, proud, welcoming. *"From the highlands of Yemen to your cup."* Invite people to sit,
slow down, and taste heritage.
