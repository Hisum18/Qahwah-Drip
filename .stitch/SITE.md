# Qahwah Drip — Site Plan

## 1. Vision
A warm, premium website for Qahwah Drip, a Yemeni coffee house ("Coffee, Teas & Desserts").
Show the heritage (Yemeni highlands, Sana'a craft), the craft (espresso + traditional qahwah),
and the space (marble, brass, mashrabiya arches, bistro patio). Invite people to visit.
